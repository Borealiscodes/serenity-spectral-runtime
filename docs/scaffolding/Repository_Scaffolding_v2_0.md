## ⭐ Why this expansion is correct

### **1. Teaching tiles need their own directory geometry**  
The Pedagogy Bridge defines:

- orientation  
- exploration  
- deepening  
- interaction  
- synthesis  

These are **tiles**, not paragraphs.  
Tiles must live in **tile directories**, not in monolithic docs.

---

### **2. Each tile must be reversible and NDH‑safe**  
Tile isolation prevents:

- tone drift  
- axis misalignment  
- codex gravity violations  
- sequencing collapse  

This is why each tile gets its own folder.

---

### **3. The Teaching Surface is a manifold**  
A manifold is composed of **charts**.  
Your charts are **tiles**.  
Therefore the repo must expose tile charts explicitly.

---

### **4. Examples must be lane‑specific**  
You now have:

- spectral geometry lane  
- runtime/envelope lane  
- emblem/visual grammar lane  

Each lane needs its own example directory.

---

### **5. This scaffolding aligns with Roadmap v2.0**  
Roadmap v2.0 explicitly calls for:

- accessible examples  
- pedagogy modules  
- multi‑modal learning  
- tile‑based sequencing  

This scaffolding implements those requirements.
