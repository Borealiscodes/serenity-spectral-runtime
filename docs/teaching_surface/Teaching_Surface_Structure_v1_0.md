## ⭐ 2 — Mode Manifold

The Teaching Surface is built on the **Omnibus three‑mode manifold**:

- **Psychonaut Mode:**  
  **Label:** Intuition & atmosphere  
  **Function:** Introduce concepts via metaphor, imagery, and gentle narrative.  
  **Usage:** First contact; low‑pressure exploration.

- **Architect Mode:**  
  **Label:** Structure & constitution  
  \**Function:** Show diagrams, schemas, envelopes, and topology of the runtime.  
  **Usage:** Bridge from intuition to formal structure.

- **Developer Mode:**  
  **Label:** Mechanics & payloads  
  **Function:** Present examples, JSON payloads, solver/renderer flows.  
  **Usage:** Hands‑on, step‑by‑step interaction.

Each teaching path must traverse **Psychonaut → Architect → Developer**, reversible at all times.

## ⭐ 4 — Sequencing & Tone Governance

Teaching sequences follow the **Pedagogy Narrative–Population Bridge**:

- **Tile roles:**  
  - orientation → exploration → deepening → interaction → synthesis  
- **Tone axes:**  
  - Attenborough ↔ macro axis  
  - Nye ↔ micro axis  
- **Rules:**  
  - one tile at a time  
  - tone must follow population role  
  - tone must bind to axis  
  - tone must remain codex‑neutral and non‑activating  

No teaching sequence may skip directly from orientation to synthesis; all transitions must be reversible.
