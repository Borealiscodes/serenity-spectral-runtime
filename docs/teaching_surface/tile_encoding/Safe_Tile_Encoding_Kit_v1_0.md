# 🟦 **Safe Tile Encoding Kit (v1.0)**  
### *Teaching Surface • Expressive‑Layer • NDH‑External • Tile‑Design‑Ready*

---

## ⭐ 1 — Identity Block (Safe)

Every tile begins with a simple identity block:

```
Tile-Class: TeachingSurfaceTile
Version: 1.0
Altitude: A0–A2 (Expressive Layer)
Lane: <choose lane>
Mode: <choose mode>
Interaction-Surface: <choose surface>
```

### Allowed lanes  
- **Spectral Geometry Basics**  
- **Runtime & Envelope**  
- **Emblem & Visual Grammar**  

### Allowed modes  
- **🌌 Psychonaut**  
- **📐 Architect**  
- **🧪 Developer**

### Allowed interaction surfaces  
- **🌲 Forest Board**  
- **🪷 Zen Board**  
- **🗒️ Sticky‑Notes**

---

## ⭐ 2 — Tile Purpose (Safe)

A tile must declare one of the five Teaching Surface purposes:

- **🧭 Orientation** — gentle introduction  
- **🔍 Exploration** — branching questions  
- **🏗️ Deepening** — structured understanding  
- **🤝 Interaction** — expressive engagement  
- **🌅 Synthesis** — integration and closure  

Each purpose has a palette, emotional geometry, and stability profile.

---

---

## ⭐ 4 — Tile Emotional Geometry (Safe)

Choose one:

- **🫶 Safe**  
- **🙂 Mild**  
- **🌈 Aesthetic**  
- **⚪ Neutral**

Tiles should default to **Safe** or **Neutral** unless intentionally expressive.

---

## ⭐ 5 — Tile Accessibility Anchors (Safe)

Every tile must include:

- **🧘 Cognitive Safe**  
- **🔀 Multi‑Modal**  
- **🛡️ NDH‑External**  
- **🔄 Reversible**

These anchors ensure Teaching Surface tiles remain posture‑safe.

---

## ⭐ 6 — Tile Stability Ecology (Safe)

Choose one stability profile:

- **⛳ High Stability**  
- **🏔️ Very High Stability**  
- **➖ Drift Neutral**  
- **↘️ Drift Low**

Teaching Surface tiles should never exceed **High Stability**.

---

## ⭐ 7 — Tile Palette (Safe)

Tiles inherit palettes from **Expanded Hex Palettes v2.0**.

### Example: Orientation Tile Palette  
```
orientation_soft_gold:      #E8D89A
orientation_mint_whisper:   #BEE8C4
orientation_sky_mist:       #C7DCEB
```

Each tile type has a palette defined in the v2.0 expansion.

---

## ⭐ 8 — Tile Interaction Surface (Safe)

Tiles may embed one of the three interaction surfaces:

### 🌲 Forest Board  
Concept organization, gentle previews.

### 🪷 Zen Board  
Expressive cognition, soft reminders.

### 🗒️ Sticky‑Notes  
Micro‑directive workspace, question clustering.

Interaction surfaces are **expressive‑layer only** and **NDH‑external**.

---

## ⭐ 9 — Tile ASCII Diagram Rules (Safe)

Allowed:

- soft boxes  
- reversible arrows  
- gentle stacks  
- expressive‑layer diagrams  

Disallowed:

- routing arrows  
- radiance  
- hierarchy  
- orbital paths
