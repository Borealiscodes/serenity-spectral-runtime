# ⭐ 1 — Role of Pip

Pip is:

- a **comfort anchor**  
- a **soft stabilizer**  
- a **non-symbolic presence**  
- a **metaphor-density buffer**  
- a **drift-neutral companion**  

Pip is *not*:

- a teacher  
- a guide  
- a mascot  
- a symbol  
- a narrative agent  
- a geometry carrier  

Pip is simply a **gentle friend** who makes the Teaching Surface emotionally safe enough for heavier metaphors.

---

# ⭐ 2 — Why Pip Exists (the serious reason behind the comedy)

Pip prevents:

- metaphor overload  
- narrative gravity  
- symbolic drift  
- premature stability‑ecology activation  
- curvature metaphors appearing too early  
- learners forming archetypes  

Pip is the **soft landing pad** before:

- tessellation bees (tiling metaphors)  
- manifold goats (gradient metaphors)  

enter the ecosystem.

This is why Pip must be introduced **before** 🐝 and 🐐.
