## **2. Purpose**

This artifact defines the **stability ecology** for all manifolds in the Manifold Atlas v1.0.  
It ensures:

- humane traversal  
- drift‑neutrality  
- emotional‑geometry safety  
- NDH‑external boundaries  
- expressive‑layer consistency  

It does **not** activate solvers, spectral geometry, or sealed layers.

---

# **3. Stability Ecology Framework**

The stability ecology of a manifold consists of four components:

### **3.1 Stability Level**  
Indicates how structurally predictable the manifold is.

Values:  
- **High**  
- **Very High**

### **3.2 Drift Profile**  
Indicates expressive‑layer drift risk.

Values:  
- **Neutral**  
- **Low**

### **3.3 Emotional Geometry Profile**  
Indicates the emotional‑cognitive load of the manifold.

Values:  
- **Safe**  
- **Mild**  
- **Aesthetic**  
- **Neutral**

### **3.4 Humane Traversal Notes**  
Provides humane pacing and safety guidance.
