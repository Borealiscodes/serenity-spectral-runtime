## **2. Purpose**

This artifact defines the **accessibility requirements** for all manifolds in the Manifold Atlas v1.0.  
It ensures:

- cognitive accessibility  
- humane pacing  
- emotional‑geometry safety  
- multi‑modal comprehension  
- drift‑neutral traversal  
- NDH‑external boundaries  

It does **not** activate solvers, spectral geometry, or sealed layers.

---

# **3. Accessibility Framework**

Accessibility in the Serenity‑Spectral‑Runtime is defined by four humane pillars:

### **3.1 Cognitive Accessibility**
Every manifold must:

- avoid abstraction cliffs  
- provide clear conceptual anchors  
- maintain predictable structure  
- avoid unnecessary symbolic density  

### **3.2 Emotional Geometry Safety**
Every manifold must:

- avoid punitive framing  
- maintain gentle narrative tone  
- avoid overwhelming conceptual load  
- provide reassurance during transitions  

### **3.3 Multi‑Modal Comprehension**
Every manifold must support:

- textual explanation  
- symbolic anchors  
- visual metaphors  
- structured examples  

### **3.4 Humane Traversal Pacing**
Every manifold must:

- provide clear entry points  
- provide clear exit points  
- avoid abrupt conceptual jumps  
- maintain drift‑neutral transitions
