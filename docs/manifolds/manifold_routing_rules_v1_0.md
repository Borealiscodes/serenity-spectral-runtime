## **2. Purpose**

These routing rules define **how designers traverse between manifolds** in the Manifold Atlas v1.0.  
They are:

- descriptive  
- humane  
- NDH‑external  
- non‑activating  
- drift‑neutral  
- stability‑ecology aligned  

They do **not** compute geometry, invoke solvers, or activate spectral engines.

---

## **3. Routing Principles**

### **3.1 Humane Routing**
All routing must:

- avoid abstraction cliffs  
- maintain humane pacing  
- provide clear entry and exit points  
- preserve emotional geometry safety  

### **3.2 NDH‑External Routing**
All routes must:

- avoid solver boundaries  
- avoid Laplacian activation  
- avoid VM solver adjacency  
- remain descriptive only  

### **3.3 Stability Ecology Routing**
All routes must:

- preserve stability tags  
- maintain drift‑neutrality  
- avoid destabilizing manifold transitions
